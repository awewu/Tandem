# Rheem Chinese Localization Standard

Use this reference when translating Rheem official VI, website copy, product UI, and design-system language into Chinese. The goal is not literal translation; preserve the brand's industrial confidence, reliability, efficiency, sustainability, and expert support.

## Localization Principle

Rheem Chinese should feel:

- 稳: reliable, durable, protected.
- 准: engineered, specified, documented.
- 省: efficient, energy-saving, cost-aware.
- 善: sustainable, responsible, future-facing.
- 通: continuous hot water, expert support, logistics, and service paths.

Avoid consumer-flashy language. Do not turn Rheem into "红色科技感" or a generic SaaS brand.

## Color To Chinese Tone

| Token | Use | Chinese tone | Good words | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rheem Red `#E4002B` | Logo, primary CTA, urgent commercial action, active state | 果断、可靠、推进 | 稳供、确认、联系专家、提交审核、关键路径 | 激情、爆款、火力全开、网红红 |
| Blue `#1B365D` | Engineering specs, documents, professional navigation | 工程、标准、权威 | 规格、蓝本、定规、技术支持、图纸 | 炫酷、科技蓝、未来感 |
| Gray `#63666A` / `#97999B` | Work surfaces, tables, secondary copy | 中性、承压、耐看 | 参数、校核、工况、记录、状态 | 高级灰、大牌灰 |
| Teal `#4F868E` | Water/system continuity, smart routing, support ecology | 清洁、流动、系统 | 水路、恒供、联动、系统流 | 清新、治愈、薄荷 |
| Green `#789D4A` | Efficiency and sustainability | 节能、低碳、长期价值 | 低碳、节能、善度、可持续、减排 | 绿色生活方式泛化 |
| Orange `#ED8B00` | Rebates, warnings, pending checks | 提醒、机会、待确认 | 补贴、提示、待核、窗口期 | 焦虑、抢购、警报化 |

## Phrase Mapping

Use compact Chinese phrases that preserve Rheem's official rhythm:

- "Tested. Trusted. Tough." -> "经久验证，值得信赖，坚韧耐用。"
- "Powering your business with an endless supply of hot water" -> "以稳定热水，支撑商业持续运转。"
- "Lower Energy Bills. Smaller Carbon Footprint." -> "降低能耗账单，减小碳足迹。"
- "Peace of Mind Performance" -> "安心运行，性能有据。"
- "One partner. Infinite solutions." -> "一个伙伴，多元方案。"
- "A Greater Degree of Good" -> "更高一度的善意。"

## UI Copy Rules

- Prefer four-to-eight-character anchors for labels: `稳供`, `定规`, `节能`, `善度`, `文档`, `校核`, `交付`, `质保`.
- Pair poetic anchors with concrete subtitles. Example: `稳供` + `峰值热水需求覆盖 96%`.
- Use English product names and trademarks when official naming matters; add Chinese explanation around them.
- Use "专家支持" instead of vague "AI智能服务" when mirroring commercial Rheem pages.
- Use "文档查找", "规格表", "质保注册", "补贴/税惠", "查找专业人员" as utility paths.
- Keep commercial copy practical: uptime, application fit, specs, delivery, emergency support.
- Keep residential copy warm but not cute: comfort, savings, warranty, find a pro, education.

## Do Not Use

- "瑞美红" as a decorative style label without evidence.
- Fake Chinese lockups inside the logo.
- "智慧生态赋能", "极致科技美学", "重塑未来", or similar empty AI-brand language.
- Excessive four-character idioms that make the interface feel ceremonial rather than useful.
- Translating every English brand phrase word-for-word when the Chinese rhythm becomes stiff.

## Review Checklist

A localized Rheem UI passes when:

- Chinese text still sounds like a durable equipment brand.
- Color choices and text tone reinforce each other.
- Every poetic phrase has a concrete operational meaning nearby.
- Commercial pages emphasize support, specification, availability, documentation, and uptime.
- Residential pages emphasize comfort, savings, warranty, expert installation, and education.
