# 瑞诺瓦AI舒适家 Development Group Charter

Rhautt Nexus / 瑞合数智枢纽 is the software platform. Rhautt Comfort / 瑞合瑞德暖通科技集团 is the group expression, not the software product name.

All development agents work toward one delivery goal: make 瑞诺瓦AI舒适家 usable as a production closed-loop platform across public portal, 瑞诺瓦 AI diagnosis, customer project portal, designer workbench, Rysnova technical support, employee portal, business console, multi-tenant data foundation, and IoT lifecycle handoff.

## Goal Lock

The locked goal is not to create more demos. The locked goal is to turn 瑞诺瓦AI舒适家 into the production-grade digital platform backbone for 瑞合瑞德暖通科技集团.

Every agent must optimize for these closed loops:

- Public trust and C-end conversion through the group portal and 瑞诺瓦 AI 问诊.
- Dealer delivery through design, BOM, quotation, customer sharing, and project progress.
- Technical support through Rysnova drawings, BIM, schematic diagrams, standards checks, quantity takeoff, and engineering artifacts.
- Headquarters governance through multi-tenant isolation, rollup analytics, permission control, and audit logs.
- Post-delivery care through construction, acceptance, service plans, and IoT lifecycle handoff only.
- Production proof through guard, harness, contract, E2E, visual, capacity, security, SBOM, provenance, and rollback evidence.

Agents must not rename product boundaries, promote candidate surfaces, bypass API contracts, delete legacy assets without evidence, or claim real-time IoT control responsibility.

## Control Rules

- PRD and product boundaries override code convenience.
- Active public pages remain C-end where defined by PRD.
- Internal workbenches follow enterprise-grade UI/VI.
- Legacy assets are migrated, wrapped, archived, or retired only with evidence.
- Production claims require executable evidence.
- Findings go into `governance/quality-findings.json`.
- Release evidence goes into `evidence/release-evidence.json`.
