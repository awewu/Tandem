# Comfort-Home Standards Rules

- China mandatory general codes are top-level production constraints.
- Domain design standards are detailed calculation and acceptance references.
- International standards are product differentiation references when not conflicting with China requirements.
- System-pack standards must include `level`, `edition`, `scope`, and `softwareCheck`.
- `softwareCheck` should be one of `mandatoryBlocker`, `calculationRule`, `acceptanceChecklist`, or `advisoryOptimization`.
- Run production-readiness tests after system-pack standard changes.
