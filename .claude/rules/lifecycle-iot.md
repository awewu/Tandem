# Lifecycle IoT Rules

- The sales/delivery platform and IoT platform meet at `/api/v2/lifecycle`.
- Handover must preserve customer, opportunity, contract, design, quote, project address, systems, devices, service plan, and IoT binding metadata.
- Installed devices must carry system, location, model, serial number where available, capabilities, and binding status.
- Smart control should be default-composed with multi-system packs.
- Run lifecycle contract tests after lifecycle or system-pack changes.
